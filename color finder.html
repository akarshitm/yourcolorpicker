How to use

  1. Upload an image — drag & drop, use the upload button, or just try the sample image already loaded.
  2. Move your cursor over it — HEX, RGB and HSL update live, with a magnified loupe for pixel-precise picking.
  3. Click any pixel to save that color, open it full screen for real-world matching, copy every value at once, find its closest name, or check text contrast.
  4. Palettes update automatically — complementary, analogous, triadic and monochromatic, generated from whatever color you're on.
  5. Saved colors stay in the side panel until you remove them — click one to bring it back.
